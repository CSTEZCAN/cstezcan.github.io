01 // FIRST-PRINCIPLES PEDIGREE

From Soil & Microcontrollers to Flat Optics & Orbital Manufacturing

4th-generation farmer • 2nd-generation Agricultural Engineer (BSc Animal Sciences, MSc Biosystems Engineering — Bursa Uludağ University).

Growing up on family farmland with an agricultural engineer father gave me an unfiltered view of physical reality: if a sensor fails at 72°C inside a greenhouse or an algorithm takes an hour to process a drone flight, it is useless in the field. Without a formal electrical engineering or computer science degree, I taught myself circuit design, C++/CUDA programming, optics, and photopolymer lithography from first principles.

In 2017, I established TARSENS R&D Ltd. as my personal engineering and commercialization vehicle. Whether designing over 50% of my PCBs in-house, machining micron-accurate wafer stages for masked UV lithography, or training generative adversarial networks on 1M+ agricultural images, I own the entire vertical stack so hardware and AI co-evolve without friction.
